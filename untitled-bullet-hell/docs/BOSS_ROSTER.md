# Boss Roster and Art Direction Proposal

This is a fresh roster proposal for the darker visual direction. The previous Mushroom Monarch, Ember Fiend, and original boss lineup are not the planned roster. The playable encounter has been restyled as an early visual-slice version of the Rimebound Warden; its current sprite is still a prototype placeholder.

Bosses, names, attacks, and stage assignments below are proposals. The stage-specific roster structure stays; the number of boss fights per run remains undecided. Combat concepts are sketches for distinct boss identities, not new systems to add all at once.

## Shared art direction

Aim for original, polished pixel art with bold silhouettes, strong outlines, expressive proportions, characterful poses, rich but controlled room detail, and colorful attacks that are easy to read. The reference is the clarity, charm, and polish of Enter the Gungeon—not its characters, sprites, animations, layouts, or projectile designs.

The mood should be eerie and mysterious rather than cute or comic. Character personality can still come through in posture and animation, but the palette, materials, and setting should feel like a strange, ancient world touched by corruption.

### Current visual audit

- `assets/` and `resources/` contain no production art assets yet.
- The player, Warden, tiles, props, and projectile sprites now have original PNG prototype art in `assets/pixel/`. Impact, torch, and some transient effects remain code-drawn. All are still visual-slice placeholders, not finished production assets.
- The project uses a 1280×720 viewport with canvas-item stretching, nearest texture filtering, and 2D transform pixel snapping.
- The main scene is a small root scene; most encounter content is created procedurally by its script.

### Proposed visual specification

Treat these as art-test targets, not locked project settings. Do not change project rendering or redraw the full game until the visual test has been reviewed.

- **Reference output:** 1280×720.
- **Initial slice scale:** Keep the existing 1280×720 viewport unchanged for now. The first hand-placed sprite test uses a 32×40 player, an 80×80 Warden, 32×32 arena tiles, and 16–24 px projectile canvases at native scale with nearest filtering.
- **Next scale test:** Compare the current native-size slice with a 640×360 logical canvas at integer 2× nearest-neighbor scale before deciding whether to change the project viewport.
- **Production target:** Aim near a 24×32 player and 64×64 standard boss, increasing boss canvas size only when the silhouette needs it.
- **Tiles and outlines:** Use clean 1 px dark outlines and a second shadow edge only where the silhouette needs it. The initial prototype tiles are 32×32 output pixels; test 16×16 source tiles at 2× in the logical-scale comparison.
- **Palette:** muted, dark environment colors with a distinct bright attack accent for each boss. Preserve the highest contrast for bullets, danger tells, and pickups.
- **Shading:** clustered pixel shapes, deliberate highlights, and restrained dithering on stone, cloth, and metal. Avoid smooth gradients that blur pixel clusters.
- **Layering:** boss, player, hazards, and projectiles must read above floor detail. Keep props at the perimeter and leave open dodge routes.
- **Animation budget:** player idle, run, staff fire, dash, and hit; boss idle, wind-up, attack, recovery, hit, and defeat; reusable projectile, hit, and power-absorption effects.
- **Placeholder rule:** label rough sketches and the existing procedural art as placeholders. Do not present them as finished production sprites.

### First visual-slice status

- Original PNG prototype assets are generated by `tools/generate_visual_slice.gd` into `assets/pixel/`.
- The slice includes the player body and staff, the Rimebound Warden, two floor tiles and a wall tile, staff/ice/frost/ember projectiles, and three perimeter props.
- `player.gd`, `boss.gd`, `tile.gd`, `projectile.gd`, and `arena_prop.gd` use the new nearest-filtered sprites. Gameplay logic and the 1280×720 viewport remain unchanged, except frost projectile hits briefly slowing the player as required by the ice boss's identity.
- The sprite shapes and colors are an early visual test, not finished art. The UI, impact animation, dash trail, and torch are still code-drawn; review them as the next visual consistency pass.
- Run `godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/generate_visual_slice.gd` to regenerate the prototype PNGs.

### Boss identity and readability rules

- Every boss gets a distinct silhouette, face or focal feature, material language, and attack palette.
- The attack must feel like something that character does: body movement or a held object should lead into the projectile/hazard.
- Telegraphs should be part of the boss's pose or leave readable marks on the floor.
- A signature status effect should support the boss fantasy, not be applied to every attack.
- Effects may decorate the action, but must not hide bullets, hazards, or safe lanes.

## Stage 1 — The Forgotten Wilds

Stage 1 introduces readable attacks and teaches the player to respond to distinct boss behaviors. The roster should feel like guardians and creatures altered by the old world's magic, not a collection of cute elemental mascots.

### The Rimebound Warden — ice golem

- **Silhouette / personality:** A hunched, broad golem built from dark glacial stone and ice grown through its armor. One arm is a massive ice maul; a dim blue light leaks from a cracked burial mask. It is deliberate and relentless.
- **Palette / materials:** Blue-black stone, dirty snow, cold cyan cracks, and a small pale-white attack highlight.
- **Signature mechanic — frost slow:** Being struck by a frost attack briefly reduces player movement speed. Standing on its temporary frost patches also slows movement; leaving the patch clears the ground effect. Keep the hit slow brief and telegraphed so it adds pressure without taking control away for long.
- **Attack ideas:**
  1. **Rimewake:** Drags its maul through the ground and advances, leaving a narrow temporary trail of slowing frost.
  2. **Grave-shard volley:** Raises the maul, marks a fan of icy lanes, then hurls jagged shards. A hit applies the brief frost slow.
  3. **Whiteout slam:** Lifts both arms and slams down, sending a ring of shards outward with broad gaps. A few floor patches remain briefly, but do not cover every route.
- **Reward direction:** A focused Frost Blast that slows a target briefly, or a defensive frost-themed passive. This is a reward choice candidate, not an approved balance.
- **Visual tell:** The mask brightens and ice climbs the maul before attacks; the frost trails and impact patches are clearly distinct from ordinary floor tiles.

### The Briar Widow — corrupted ruin-stalker

- **Silhouette / personality:** A long-limbed, low creature wrapped in root-like limbs and a ragged veil of leaves. A cracked porcelain face peers from the center. It is patient and territorial, not comic.
- **Palette / materials:** Near-black bark, muted moss, bone-colored mask, and sharp amber-green thorn accents.
- **Signature mechanic:** The Widow controls space with visible root paths and thorn clusters rather than poison clouds or generic bullet rings.
- **Attack ideas:**
  1. **Rootline:** Hooks limbs into the floor; marked lines erupt into thorn fences with clear gaps.
  2. **Widow's cast:** Flings a small set of barbed seed projectiles that spread after traveling, leaving room to dodge close to the source.
  3. **Snare bloom:** Marks a few separate floor patches; roots briefly catch or slow the player if they stand on them when they bloom.
- **Reward direction:** A short-range thorn burst or a passive that rewards careful movement.
- **Visual tell:** Limbs visibly plant before roots appear; the ground marks use pale bark colors rather than the Warden's cyan frost.

### The Cinder Reliquary — ash-bound guardian

- **Silhouette / personality:** A hunched shrine-beast carrying a cracked iron reliquary in its chest. Its lid and chained limbs open like a furnace under strain. It feels sorrowful and dangerous, not like a small fire demon.
- **Palette / materials:** Soot-black iron, ash gray, dark red-brown, and restrained ember-orange.
- **Signature mechanic:** The sealed chest is the source of each attack; opening and venting it makes the firing direction legible.
- **Attack ideas:**
  1. **Bellows breath:** Chest plates open and vent a short cone of embers after a visible inhale.
  2. **Ashfall:** Shudders and shakes cinders from above onto several marked floor spots.
  3. **Chain-drag:** Pulls its reliquary across a marked line, leaving a brief hot trail before turning back toward the player.
- **Reward direction:** A deliberate ember projectile or a small offensive bonus after a successful dodge.
- **Visual tell:** The chest glows through seams before attacks; projectiles are orange, but floor warnings use a contrasting pale ash ring.

## Stage 2 — The Fallen Kingdom

Stage 2 combines learned attack shapes and asks players to reposition more deliberately. Bosses remain visually readable in rooms with richer architecture.

### The Gallows Knight

- **Silhouette / personality:** A tall, headless knight whose enormous execution blade is chained to one arm; a small lantern hangs where its head should be.
- **Palette / materials:** Tarnished iron, faded blue cloth, bone-white blade edge, and a dim green lantern.
- **Signature mechanic:** The knight commits to slow blade paths that split or divide the arena; it punishes remaining in a marked lane.
- **Reward direction:** A short shadow-step or a defensive counter after a well-timed dash.

### The Mire Abbot

- **Silhouette / personality:** A robed, half-sunken guardian whose bell-shaped shell and trailing reeds conceal a swollen, watchful face.
- **Palette / materials:** Peat brown, black-green water, tarnished brass, and sickly pale lantern light.
- **Signature mechanic:** Creates shallow, temporary mire zones that reduce movement, then pressures the player with bell-sent projectile waves.
- **Reward direction:** A brief area-control pulse or a passive that improves movement through hazards.

### The Crown Engine

- **Silhouette / personality:** A squat, ancient siege construct with a rotating crown of stone plates and one exposed rune chamber.
- **Palette / materials:** Dark basalt, rusted gold, and a sharp white-gold rune core.
- **Signature mechanic:** Repositions slowly while rotating armor plates redirect or block some attacks; the player attacks during its exposed recovery.
- **Reward direction:** A charged arcane shot or a modest staff upgrade.

## Stage 3 — The Shattered Expanse

Stage 3 is a late-run roster proposal. Attacks may combine earlier lessons, but every sequence still needs a visible tell and a deliberate escape route.

### The Starved Comet

- **Silhouette / personality:** A fallen celestial body wrapped in a ragged mantle and a few orbiting stone fragments; a dim, hungry core breaks its outline.
- **Palette / materials:** Deep indigo, violet-black stone, and a tightly controlled magenta-white core.
- **Signature mechanic:** Alters projectile routes with a short, clearly telegraphed pull before releasing them outward.
- **Reward direction:** A temporary gravity well or projectile-control effect.

### The Tempest Pilgrim

- **Silhouette / personality:** A tall, cloaked wanderer fused with a broken storm shrine, carrying a forked staff that splits the sky.
- **Palette / materials:** Storm gray, muted blue cloth, and bright white-blue lightning.
- **Signature mechanic:** Calls lightning along marked ground paths, then walks through those paths to create a moving safe corridor.
- **Reward direction:** A targeted lightning strike or improved dash recovery.

### The Hollow Eclipse

- **Silhouette / personality:** A broad, ceremonial guardian whose mask is a dark circular void framed by a ring of broken stone.
- **Palette / materials:** Chalk stone, black-violet shadow, and thin gold markings.
- **Signature mechanic:** Alternates between attacks that fill the outer arena and attacks that fill the center, making the safe region shift predictably.
- **Reward direction:** A versatile arcane active power.

## Recommended roster development order

1. Review the current **Rimebound Warden** art and frost-hit slow. Keep hit slow short and confirm the player can still dodge.
2. Compare the current native-scale slice with the proposed 640×360 logical canvas; adopt a scale only after checking sprite and projectile readability.
3. If the Warden visual and roster direction are approved, implement the **Briar Widow** as a second Stage 1 roster choice; then add the **Cinder Reliquary**.
4. Add Stage 2 and Stage 3 candidates after the roster selector and first-stage art are working.

## Decisions to approve

- Names, silhouettes, and palettes for the three Stage 1 candidates.
- Whether temporary frost ground should be added alongside the implemented frost-hit slow.
- Whether the proposed 640×360 logical grid at integer 2× scale reads correctly in the visual slice.
- Stage 2/3 candidates and how many bosses a run selects from each stage.
- The actual ability/passive rewards and their balance.
